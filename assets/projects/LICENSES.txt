Agent Observer terminal stills

The wide and compact Observer stills in this portfolio are byte-identical copies
of the corresponding Fleet Plus assets, with no additional resize or crop. They
show the released Agent Observer CLI using fictional cached sources. The capture
renderer used the system's Noto Sans Mono Regular font and rasterized its glyphs
into the images; no font binary is distributed here. The exact release, renderer,
recipe, dependencies, font hash and fixture are recorded in Fleet Plus
assets/provenance.json.
